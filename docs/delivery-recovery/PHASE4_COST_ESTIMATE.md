# Historical cost model

The demonstration used a GBP20 gross budget policy and GBP12 operational stop threshold, not an account-wide enforced spend cap. Compute was stopped during pauses; project billable infrastructure was deleted after recovery verification.

`phase4-cost-estimate.json`, `phase4-pricing-provenance.json` and `scripts/phase4_cost_estimate.py` retain the dated development estimate methodology. They are not current price advice or measured billing. Historical estimate: approximately GBP0.0381/hour gross for the instance plus its public IPv4 while assigned; stopped storage approximately GBP0.0846/day. Refresh rates, tax/exchange assumptions and inventory before any future deployment.

No remaining project billable resource was recorded after cleanup. This is scoped inventory evidence, not an assertion of an immediate zero AWS bill or zero account-wide spend.
